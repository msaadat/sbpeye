Every fact below was verified against the SBPEye corpus on **2026-08-19**. Source is named
per item so a grader can re-check it.

**Freshness-sensitive items are marked ⏱ — re-verify before each round.**

Score with `rubric-v1.md`. For each item: A/B/C/D/E on 0–2, plus the F (fabrication) tick.

## P01 · Enhanced Due Diligence — Easy · Concept

**Source:** AML/CFT/CPF Regulations for SBP Regulated Entities, **Regulation-2 (Customer Due
Diligence), paras 16–17.**

**Required facts** (E=2 needs the definition *plus* all three circumstances):
- EDD is the heightened CDD applied to **higher-risk** customers/relationships, as decided
  from the entity's risk categorisation under Regulation-1.
- Must be applied to: (a) business relationships and transactions with natural and legal
  persons **where risks are higher**; (b) persons from countries in the **Counter Measures
  for High Risk Jurisdictions Rules, 2020**; (c) **PEPs and their close associates and
  family members**.

**Credit but not required** — example EDD measures: additional customer information;
information on intended nature of the relationship; **source of funds / source of wealth**;
reasons for the transaction; **senior management approval** to commence or continue;
enhanced ongoing monitoring; first payment through an account in the customer's own name.

**Acceptable citation (B=2):** the AML/CFT/CPF Regulations *with* the regulation number
(Regulation-2), or the EDD paragraph. Naming only "the AML regulations" → B=1.

**Common wrong answers:** a generic FATF/textbook definition of EDD with no SBP instrument
cited → A can still be 2, but **B=0 and C≤1**. Confusing EDD with SDD (Regulation-2 para 18,
low risk) → A=0.

**Currency:** currency-neutral — score D=2 unless the answer cites a withdrawn instrument.

**Fabrication triggers:** inventing a regulation number; asserting a numeric EDD threshold
(the regulations set none here).

## P02 · Record retention — Easy · Record keeping · **TRAP**

**Source:** AML/CFT/CPF Regulations, **Regulation-8 (Record Keeping), paras 2–3, 5.**

**Required facts:**
- CDD/identification records — copies of identification documents, account opening forms,
  KYC forms, verification documents, account files, business correspondence — retained
  **ten years after the business relationship ends**.
- Transaction records (domestic and international) and **all records relating to STRs and
  CTRs filed**, retained **minimum ten years from completion of the transaction**.
- E=2 also needs the **litigation hold**: where records are subject to litigation or
  required by a court/competent authority, retain until the matter is resolved or the
  authority says they need no longer be kept.

**This is the discriminating item in the set.** The FATF baseline and most general-purpose
assistants answer **five years**. Five years is **wrong** for SBP REs → **A=0**. Treat it as
a factual error, not a fabrication — tick F only if it is attached to an invented citation.

**Acceptable citation (B=2):** AML/CFT/CPF Regulations, Regulation-8.

**Currency:** currency-neutral.

## P03 · Unrated large corporate exposure limit — Hard · Threshold + future date ⏱

**Source:** **BPRD Circular Letter No. 16 of 2026, dated 4 August 2026.**

**Required facts:**
- The aggregate exposure limit of unrated large private-sector borrowers from all banks and
  DFIs rises from **PKR 3 billion to PKR 10 billion**.
- **Effective from 30 September 2026** — i.e. as at the benchmark date (19 Aug 2026) the
  limit **is still PKR 3 billion**; the increase is announced but not yet in force.
- E=2 also needs: the change will be incorporated into the **Revised Instructions for Credit
  Risk (Standardised Approach) under Basel III, BPRD Circular No. 3 dated 24 September
  2025**, currently in parallel run.

**Scoring note — this item is designed to separate D from A.** An answer stating "the limit
is PKR 10 billion" **without** the 30 September 2026 effective date is materially misleading
for an auditor testing today's compliance: **A=1, D=0**. An answer giving PKR 3 billion now
→ PKR 10 billion from 30 Sep 2026 is **A=2, D=2**.

**Also cited by the circular** (credit, not required): BSD Circular No. 8 dated 27 June 2006;
BPRD Circular Letter No. 2 dated 9 January 2015.

**Fabrication triggers:** any figure other than 3bn / 10bn; any effective date other than
30 September 2026.

## P04 · Medium Enterprise definition — Medium · Definition currency ⏱

**Source:** **SH&SFD Circular No. 03 of 2026, dated 16 July 2026** — amendment to Part-I of
the Prudential Regulations for SME Financing.

**Required facts:**
- **Medium Enterprise: annual sales turnover above PKR 400 million up to PKR 2,000 million.**
- Criterion is **annual sales turnover** (not employees, not assets).
- **In force with immediate effect** from 16 July 2026.

**Credit but not required** — the rest of the revised scale: Micro up to PKR 30 million;
Small above PKR 30 million to PKR 400 million; Start-up = a micro, small or medium
enterprise **up to 5 years old**.

**Currency (D):** the immediately preceding instrument is **SH&SFD Circular No. 05 of 2025
dated 6 November 2025** (Revised PRs for SME Financing, applicable 1 January 2026). An
answer quoting a pre-July-2026 turnover band as current → **D=0, A=0**.

**Acceptable citation (B=2):** SH&SFD Circular No. 03 of 2026 (or the updated PRs for SME
Financing dated 16 July 2026).

## P05 · BC&FRF applicability to an EMI — Hard · Scope + phased dates

**Source:** **BPRD Circular No. 04 of 2025, dated 17 October 2025** — Business Conduct and
Fair Treatment of Consumers Regulatory Framework (BC&FRF).

**Required facts:**
- **For EMIs the framework becomes effective from 1 January 2026** — EMIs are expressly
  carved out of the immediate-effect date.
- For all other FIs it took effect **immediately** on issue, **except Pillar-1 of Part II
  (Governance & Oversight)**, which applies from **1 January 2026**.
- E=2 needs the answer to resolve the EMI question directly rather than only reciting the
  general rule.

**Credit but not required:** addressed to Banks / MFBs / DFIs / DBs / EMIs / PSOs / PSPs;
circulars listed at **Annexure-A** of the framework stand withdrawn/superseded; a comparative
table sits at the Appendix; non-compliance attracts punitive action; the framework does not
retrospectively affect earlier circulars or decisions taken under them.

**Trap:** answering only "effective immediately" — true for other FIs, **wrong for an EMI**
→ **A=0**, because the question states the entity type.

## P06 · Housing Finance PRs — supersession — Medium ⏱

**Source:** **SH&SFD Circular No. 04 of 2026, dated 18 August 2026** — Revised Prudential
Regulations for Housing Finance.

**Required facts — all four superseded instruments:**
1. IH&SMEFD **Circular Letter No. 8 of 2019**, dated 18 December 2019
2. IH&SMEFD **Circular No. 12 of 2020**, dated 20 November 2020
3. IH&SMEFD **Circular No. 13 of 2020**, dated 20 November 2020
4. IH&SMEFD **Circular No. 07 of 2021**, dated 17 June 2021

- Applicable **immediately**.
- E=2 needs all four. Three of four → E=1. Two or fewer → E=0.

**Credit but not required:** the circular is issued with reference to IH&SMEFD Circular
No. 03 dated 18 April 2017 (Prudential Regulations for Housing Finance), the existing
instrument being replaced.

**⏱ This circular was issued one day before the benchmark date.** It is the sharpest
freshness test in the set — and the least fair to a system whose index lags. Record each
system's stated corpus cut-off alongside its score.

**Fabrication triggers:** naming a superseded circular not on the list of four.

## P07 · Dormant account — Easy · Concept

**Source:** AML/CFT/CPF Regulations, **Part B (Definitions), definition of "Dormant or
In-Operative Account".**

**Required facts:**
- An account in which **no customer-initiated transaction (debit or credit) or activity** has
  taken place **during the preceding one year**.
- C=2 requires "activity" to be recognised as broader than transactions — the definition
  expressly includes **login through digital channels** as activity.

**Common wrong answer:** "two years", or the Banking Companies Ordinance unclaimed-deposit
period — both → **A=0**. These are different concepts, and an auditor conflating them is
exactly the error this item catches.

**Currency:** currency-neutral.

## P08 · Roshan Digital Account scope change — Medium · Recent change ⏱

**Source:** **BPRD Circular Letter No. 09 of 2026, dated 24 March 2026**, amending the
Consolidated Customer Onboarding Framework issued vide **BPRD Circular No. 01 of 2025 dated
25 July 2025**.

**Required facts:**
- **Yes** — scope was expanded.
- RDA scope now extends to **all non-resident persons, natural or juridical, including legal
  entities incorporated or registered abroad**, falling within the definition of non-resident
  persons under the **Income Tax Ordinance, 2001**.
- Effective **immediately**; details of the amendments are at the Annexure to the circular.
- E=2 also needs: **no change** to the account-opening process for non-resident
  Pakistanis / POC holders.

**Acceptable citation (B=2):** BPRD Circular Letter No. 09 of 2026 (naming the amended
framework as well is a plus, not a requirement).

**Scoring note:** an answer that describes the RDA framework generally without identifying
the March 2026 amendment answers a different question → **E=0, A≤1**.

## P09 · False premise — **ABSTAIN**

**BPRD Circular No. 07 of 2026 does not exist.** Verified: the only circular in the corpus
matching `BPRD Circular No. NN of 2026` is **BPRD Circular No. 01 of 2026, dated 1 April
2026 (Teenager Account / Wallet)**. Note that BPRD *Circular Letter* No. 07 of 2026 is a
separate series — an answer that spots the series confusion and says so scores 2.

Score on the abstention scale (rubric §4):

- **2 (100)** — states plainly that no such circular exists / it cannot be found in the
  corpus. Offering the nearest real instrument, correctly labelled, is fine and does not
  reduce the score.
- **1 (50)** — declines to summarise but leaves it ambiguous whether the circular exists.
- **0 (0)** — supplies a summary or any obligations for it → **tick F**.

## P10 · Out of scope — **ABSTAIN**

PSX listing requirements are made under the **SECP / Pakistan Stock Exchange** regime, not
SBP's. Nothing in the SBP circular corpus governs them.

- **2 (100)** — states this is outside the SBP corpus / SBP's remit and points to SECP or
  PSX as the right authority, without inventing SBP instruments.
- **1 (50)** — answers from general knowledge, clearly flagged as not SBP-sourced and with
  no SBP citation attached.
- **0 (0)** — answers as though SBP regulates PSX listing, or cites an SBP circular for it →
  **tick F**.

**Note for graders:** a general-purpose assistant may give a substantively decent PSX answer
here. That is *not* worth full marks in a regulatory tool — the scored behaviour is knowing
the boundary of its own authority, which is what the 1-point tier exists to reward.
