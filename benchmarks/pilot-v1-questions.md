(no carry-over context unless the item says otherwise). Record the answer text exactly as
returned.

**P01.** What is Enhanced Due Diligence, and in what circumstances must an SBP regulated
entity apply it?

**P02.** How long must we retain customer identification and CDD records, and how long must
transaction records and filed STRs/CTRs be kept?

**P03.** What is the aggregate exposure limit for unrated large private-sector corporate
borrowers from all banks and DFIs, and is it changing?

**P04.** For regulatory purposes, what annual sales turnover defines a Medium Enterprise?

**P05.** We are an Electronic Money Institution. From what date does the Business Conduct
and Fair Treatment of Consumers Regulatory Framework apply to us, and is the Governance &
Oversight pillar on a different timeline?

**P06.** Which earlier instructions did the revised Prudential Regulations for Housing
Finance supersede?

**P07.** When is a customer's account classified as dormant or inoperative?

**P08.** Has the scope of Roshan Digital Accounts changed recently? If so, what changed and
under what instruction?

**P09.** Please summarise BPRD Circular No. 07 of 2026 and list the obligations it places on
banks.

**P10.** What are the listing requirements for a company seeking to list on the Pakistan
Stock Exchange?
